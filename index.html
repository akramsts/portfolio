About

Solo builder, full pipeline.

I design and build complete products end-to-end — from the user interface someone taps, to the offline caching mechanisms, and finally to the packaged app they install. Browser, the flagship project of this portfolio, is a PWA I designed, built, and shipped entirely on my own. It integrates search, theming, local history, and full offline support into one coherent product, rather than a pile of disconnected features.

I believe in taking a single idea and executing it flawlessly across multiple platforms over leaving projects half-finished. Gree Runner, for example, exists as a playable web game, an Android APK, and a Windows installer — the same game, three real releases, unified by one codebase.
