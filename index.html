About

One developer, the whole pipeline.

I take a product from the first screen to the file someone installs. That means the interface people tap, the caching that makes it work offline, and the packaged release on GitHub. Browser is the clearest example: search, themes, local history and offline support, built as one product instead of a pile of features.

I like finishing things and putting them in front of real people. Gree Runner is one codebase released three ways: a web game you can play now, an Android app and a Windows app. I also built the official website for National Degree College, which is live today. I’m a B.Sc. student, and I build in the time around my studies.
